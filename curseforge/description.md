**Carry On Bug Fix** is an addon for Carry On.

## Features

- **Carry riding entities as a group**: when you pick up a boat with a villager inside, a minecart with a mob, or a stacked tower of mobs, all the passengers come with it.
- **Fixed the carry key**: fixed the bug where the carry key could not be bound to a mouse button, and where pressing the key had no effect after binding it.

## Config

`carry_on_bug_fix-common.toml`

- `passengerStack.enabled`: turns group carrying on or off (default `true`). When off, Carry On behaves as it normally does.
- `passengerStack.maxDepth`: the most layers allowed in a group, counting the bottom vehicle (1-3, default 3).
- `passengerStack.maxEntities`: the most entities allowed in a group (1-8, default 8).

## Requirements

- Carry On

**Carry On Bug Fix** 是 Carry On 的附属模组。

## 功能

- **整组搬运**：抱起装着村民的船、坐着生物的矿车，或者叠在一起的生物塔时，所有乘客都会一起带走。
- **修复搬运按键**：修复了搬运按键无法绑定鼠标按键，以及绑定按键后怎么按都无效的问题。

## 配置

`carry_on_bug_fix-common.toml`

- `passengerStack.enabled`：整组搬运开关（默认 `true`）。关闭后恢复 Carry On 原本的行为。
- `passengerStack.maxDepth`：一组最多几层，包括最底下的载具（1-3，默认 3）。
- `passengerStack.maxEntities`：一组最多几个实体（1-8，默认 8）。

## 前置

- Carry On
